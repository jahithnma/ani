HOW TO PLAY

The story: a family feud has turned into a war. Pick a side - Parents or Sons.

Key card: one fighter on each team secretly carries a KEY CARD. Nobody knows who. When the carrier falls, the card appears. Grab the enemy's card and reach the gate to escape to the next level. If the enemy grabs your team's card and reaches the gate first, you lose!

Souls: on the next level, the aathmas (souls) of everyone who fell come back to chase you. Shoot them and run along the path to the gate. The more fell before, the more souls return...

Every level has a twist: darkness, broken bridges, fake key cards, a closing gate, sandstorms, slippery ice, meteors and a final boss.
